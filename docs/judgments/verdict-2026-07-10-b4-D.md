### libs/magic-ast/Parsing/Parsers/Triggered/Rules/SubtypeYouControlDealsCombatDamageToPlayerConditionRule.cs
**Verdict:** FAIL
**Issue:** Wrong combat-step citation — cites CR 509 as the "Combat Damage Step" but 509 is the Declare Blockers Step.
**Rule citation:** CR 509 (cited) vs CR 510 (correct)
**Rule text:**
> CR 509 = "Declare Blockers Step"
> CR 510 = "Combat Damage Step" — "First, the active player announces how each attacking creature assigns its combat damage…"
**What the AST node says:**
> `/// CR 509 (Combat Damage Step): combat damage assignment is the game event.`
**Why this misrepresents the rule:** The node models a "deals combat damage to a player" trigger. Combat damage is dealt in the Combat Damage Step (CR 510), not the Declare Blockers Step (CR 509). rules-structure.json contradicts the comment's claim that 509 is the Combat Damage Step — a contradictory citation, not mere subrule imprecision. (The load-bearing CR 603.2 triggered-ability cite is correct; the fixture itself carries no citation and is faithfully modeled.)
**Suggested fix:** Change `CR 509 (Combat Damage Step)` to `CR 510 (Combat Damage Step)` in the doc-comment. One-line fix; re-render.

Prior judges flagged it as an anti-pattern but explicitly deferred it as out-of-batch systemic remediation, **not** a HALT (verdict-2026-05-25 §59: "Pre-existing convention… not in batch 3 scope but should be tracked"; verdict-2026-05-26-batch25 praises `IsColorless:true` as the correct alternative). Consistent with that precedent this shard does **not** FAIL StoneIdolTrap on the `["C"]` encoding — but it is re-surfaced here for the tracked corpus-wide remediation pass (empty-Colors / `TokenDefinition.IsColorless`).
