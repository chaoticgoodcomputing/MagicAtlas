## F4 — "This creature can't attack or block alone" static (Loyal Pegasus) — opus, JUDGE
- **Signal:** `StaticAbilityParser.Parse`, DY 6. "Flying" parses; "This creature can't attack or block alone." is unparsed.
- **CR 508.1** (verbatim, excerpt): "First, the active player declares attackers. This turn-based action doesn't use the stack…" **CR 509.1** (verbatim, excerpt): "First, the defending player declares blockers…" — "alone" means the restriction applies unless another creature also attacks/blocks.
- **AST:** there are existing `CantAttackEffect` / `CantBlockEffect` nodes, but neither carries an "alone" qualifier. Decide (you own it): a shared "alone"/"unless another also does" qualifier on those, or a small dedicated node. The clause is ONE restriction covering both attack and block "alone". Keep it descriptive — the "unless another attacks/blocks" condition is what the text says, not engine resolution.
- **Anti-patterns:** don't free-text "alone"; don't model two unrelated effects if the text is one restriction; don't import turn-state/engine semantics.
