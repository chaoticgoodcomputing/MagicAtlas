## FAIL verdicts

### controlled-have-keyword-grant (F12)
**Verdict:** FAIL
**Issue:** the `MayCastFromTopOfLibraryRule` generalization mislabels a corpus sibling.
**Rule citation:** CR 205.2a (the card types)
**Rule text:** > "The card types are artifact, battle, conspiracy, creature, dungeon, enchantment, instant, kindred, land, phenomenon, plane, planeswalker, scheme, sorcery, and vanguard." ("noncreature" is not among them.)
**What the matcher does:** broadening `^You may cast artifact spells and colorless spells from the top of your library.$` to `^You may cast (?<a>[A-Za-z]+) spells and (?<b>[A-Za-z]+) spells from the top of your library.$`, with `BuildSpellFilter` mapping a lowercase word to `CardTypes=[word]`. Corpus simulation of the two-word-each form matches exactly three cards: **Sigarda, Font of Blessings** (target — "Angel spells and Human spells" → Subtypes, correct), **Mystic Forge** (original — "artifact spells and colorless spells", unchanged output), and **Madame Web, Clairvoyant** — "Spider spells and **noncreature** spells" → the lowercase branch emits `CardTypes:["noncreature"]`.
**Why this misrepresents the rules:** "noncreature" is a negation, not a card type. The codebase's own `ObjectFilter.cs` documents the convention — a "non-[type]" qualifier is `CardTypes=["card"]` + `ExcludedCardTypes=["type"]` (e.g. nonland → `CardTypes=["card"]`+`ExcludedCardTypes=["land"]`). Encoding it as a literal `CardTypes` entry is a wrong-label the same class as the free-text type strings the doctrine forbids, and it is invisible to downstream consumers. `MayPlayFromTopOfLibraryRule`/`MayPlayLandsFromTopOfLibraryRule` do not claim Madame Web's two-class line (they're anchored to other shapes), so the generalized rule (priority 940) is its sole owner. No fixture covers Madame Web and the edge-diff gate is offline, so nothing else catches it.
**Suggested fix:** in `BuildSpellFilter`, detect a leading `non` on a lowercase word and split it into `CardTypes=["card"]`+`ExcludedCardTypes=[stem]` (matching the ObjectFilter convention), or explicitly decline the `noncreature` case so the line stays unclaimed rather than mislabeled.

The co-committed `ControlledFilterHaveKeywordListRule` ("[filter] you control have [kw list]") is itself CLEAN: simulated over the corpus, its guards correctly DECLINE every Creature/Creatures/Attacking-token sibling (Combine Chrysalis, Starry-Eyed Skyrider, Aven Wind Guide, Teysa Karlov, Neyali, Bone-Cairn Butcher, Brudiclad, Echoing Assault, Gothmog, Phabine, Roar of Resistance — all fall through to their existing owners), and the shapes it does own are correct: the Other-permanents grants (Sigarda target, plus Avacyn / The Walls of Ba Sing Se) and the Zombie-token grants (Gleaming Overseer target, plus Eternal Skylord / Dreadhorde Twins / Vizier of the Scorpion). The three worker-cited regressions (CombineChrysalis, SomberwaldBeastmaster, StarryEyedSkyrider) are all correctly declined. The FAIL is entirely on the MayCast generalization, but it lands on the same branch, so the branch HALTs.
