## Family A — "Add one mana of the chosen color" (chosen-color mana ability) — JUDGED

- **Failure signal:** `UnparsedEffect/null`. Cluster template `<TAP>: <SUBTYPE> <N> mana of the chosen color.` fY=13, dY=10, 16 cards.
- **Cards:** Coldsteel Heart (artifact), Shimmerdrift Vale (land). Both: `{T}: Add one mana of the chosen color.` The `enters tapped` + `As ~ enters, choose a color` lines already parse (`ChooseColorEffect`) — only the mana line fails.
- **Relevant CR:**
  - **605.1a:** "An activated ability is a mana ability if it meets all of the following criteria: it doesn't require a target (see rule 115.6), it could add mana to a player's mana pool when it resolves, and it's not a loyalty ability."
- **AST in scope:** `AddManaEffect` (Resource/AddManaEffect.cs) exists with `Mana` (string), `AnyColor` (bool), `SpendRestriction` (string?), `Amount` (Quantity?). `ChooseColorEffect` (Keyword/) models the *declaration* "choose a color." The gap: `AddManaEffect` cannot express "**of the chosen color**" — a back-reference to a prior color choice. `AddManaEffectRule` (Activated/Rules/) exists and is the place to wire it.
- **Expected generalization:** a structural representation of "the chosen color" as the produced mana's color source — parallel to the existing `AnyColor` flag (e.g. a second flag/enum value, your call). Mirror `ChooseColorEffect`'s doctrine: MAST records the *reference* only, NOT the producer→consumer link.
- **Anti-patterns:** do NOT free-text "chosen" into `Mana`. Do NOT model the link to the ChooseColorEffect (engine territory). Watch the known greedy-`AddManaEffectRule` failure mode (memory `project_mast_triage_false_coverage`): the gold must show the chosen-color reference *structurally*, not swallowed into the `Mana` scalar.

- **Anti-patterns:** don't invent a new effect — `ReturnToHandEffect` already exists. Don't conflate with the triggered death-variant `ReturnSelfToHandOnDeathTriggeredRule`.

## Family G — Keyrune/Monument animate: "becomes a P/T … creature with [keyword] until end of turn" — JUDGED

- **Failure signal:** `UnparsedEffect/null`. Template `<COST>: <SUBTYPE> <TYPE> becomes a <N>/<N> <COLOR> and <COLOR> <SUBTYPE> <TYPE> <TYPE> with <KW> until end of turn.` fY=10, dY=10, 10 cards.
- **Cards:** Azorius Keyrune (`{W}{U}: This artifact becomes a 2/2 white and blue Bird artifact creature with flying until end of turn.`), Gruul Keyrune (`{R}{G}: This artifact becomes a 3/2 red and green Beast artifact creature with trample until end of turn.`). `{T}: Add {W} or {U}` mana ability already parses.
- **Relevant CR:** there is no single keyword action for "becomes a creature" — it is a **continuous effect** that sets characteristics for a duration. Descriptively, the line sets: power/toughness, colors, a creature subtype, the artifact creature card type, and grants a keyword ability, "until end of turn." Cite the characteristic rules you actually rely on (205 types, 208 P/T, 105 colors) and treat layer/timestamp ordering (613) as engine territory.
- **AST in scope:** building blocks exist — `DefinePTEffect`, `ChangeColorEffect`, `ChangeSubtypeEffect`, `GainAbilityEffect` (Modification/), plus `Duration`. **No single "becomes a creature"/animate effect exists.** Your doctrinal call: a new `BecomesCreatureEffect` (composite node bundling P/T + colors + subtypes + added type + granted keywords + duration) vs. a `CompositeEffect` of the existing primitives. Prefer the descriptively-faithful shape: oracle text says one thing ("becomes a [full spec] until end of turn"), so a single node is likely truer than a bag of primitives — justify whichever you pick in the doc-comment. This is an `Effect` (allowed), not a new trait interface (would be a STOP).
- **Expected generalization:** `{cost}: This <self> becomes a <P/T> <colors> <subtypes> <added-type> creature with <keywords> until end of turn`. Self subject; "until end of turn" duration.
- **Anti-patterns:** don't model layers/timestamps. Don't introduce a new `Effect` trait interface (HITL). Don't leave any sub-part (P/T, colors, subtype, keyword) as free text.

## Family H — Circle/Rune of Protection: "The next time a [color] source of your choice would deal damage to you this turn, prevent that damage" — JUDGED

- **Failure signal:** `UnparsedEffect/null`. Template `<COST>: <SUBTYPE> next time a <COLOR> source of your choice would deal damage to you this turn, prevent that damage.` fY=10, dY=10, 10 cards.
- **Cards:** Circle of Protection: Red (`{1}: The next time a red source of your choice would deal damage to you this turn, prevent that damage.` — single line, cleanest), Rune of Protection: Red (`{W}: …prevent that damage.` + `Cycling {2}` which already parses).
- **Relevant CR:**
  - **615.8:** "Some prevention effects generated by the resolution of a spell or ability refer to the next time a specific source would deal damage. These effects prevent the next instance of damage from that source, regardless of how much damage that is. Once an instance of damage from that source has been prevented, any subsequent instances of damage that would be dealt by that source are dealt normally."
  - **615.2:** "Many prevention effects apply to damage from a source. See rule 609.7."
- **AST in scope:** `PreventDamageEffect` (Damage/, discriminator `preventDamage`) exists; `PreventNextNDamageToAnyTargetEffectRule` exists for a *different* shape (prevent next N to a target). This is the **Circle-of-Protection one-shot shield**: prevent the **next instance** of damage from a **source of your choice** matching a color, to **you**, **this turn**. Extend `PreventDamageEffect` (and add an activated rule) to carry: a source filter (color), the recipient (you), "next instance" semantics, and a "this turn" duration.
- **Expected generalization:** `{cost}: prevent the next damage from a [color] source of your choice to you this turn`. Descriptive only.
- **Anti-patterns:** prevention is CR-615 prevention, NOT a replacement effect — do NOT reach for `ReplacementEffect`/`ReplacementEvent`. If modeling this honestly forces replacement-effect ordering or a new trait interface, **STOP and report** (engine-boundary). "Source of your choice" is a player decision — record the reference, not the resolution.
