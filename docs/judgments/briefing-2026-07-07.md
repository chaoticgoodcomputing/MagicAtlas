## F1 — Life-gain replacement ("gain that much life plus N instead")

**Cluster:** rank 7, fusedScore 37.0, comboBlockedCount 165, mass 19,368. **Dominant bail:**
`StaticAbilityParser.Parse`. **Card (exemplar):** Pest Rescuer.

**Failure signal:** the static line `If you would gain life, you gain that much life plus 1 instead.`
falls through `StaticAbilityParser.Parse` — no rule recognizes a life-gain replacement.

**Mechanic:** a **replacement effect** that watches the life-gain event and increases the amount. It is
a continuous static ability, not triggered.

**Relevant rules (verbatim from `rules-structure.json`):**
- **CR 614.1** — "Some continuous effects are replacement effects. Like prevention effects (see rule 615),
  replacement effects apply continuously as events happen—they aren't locked in ahead of time. Such
  effects watch for a particular event that would happen and completely or partially replace it…"
- **CR 119.3** — "If an effect causes a player to gain life or lose life, that player's life total is
  adjusted accordingly."

**Likely AST scope:** a `ReplacementEffect`/`StaticAbility` shape whose event is a life-gain and whose
result adds a fixed quantity ("plus 1"). Check GLOSSARY.md for an existing replacement/`ReplaceEvent`
node and a life-gain event before inventing one. The "plus N" is a scaling quantity on the replaced
amount. **Anti-pattern:** do not model this as a triggered "whenever you gain life" ability (it's a
replacement, CR 614.1) and do not leave the amount as free text.

**Gold Input (authoritative — verbatim from corpus, use as-is):**

  At the beginning of each upkeep, if you don't control a Pest creature token, create a 1/1 black and green Pest creature token with "When this token dies, you gain 1 life."

## F2 — Skip your draw step

**Cluster:** rank 8, fusedScore 33.8, comboBlockedCount 96, mass 11,634. **Dominant bail:**
`StaticAbilityParser.Parse`. **Card (exemplar):** Symbiotic Deployment.

**Failure signal:** `Skip your draw step.` falls through `StaticAbilityParser.Parse` — no rule for a
step-skip replacement.

**Mechanic:** skipping a step is a **replacement effect** (per CR, "Skip [x]" ≡ "Instead of doing [x],
do nothing"). Here the skipped event is the controller's draw step.

**Relevant rules (verbatim):**
- **CR 614.10** — "An effect that causes a player to skip an event, step, phase, or turn is a replacement
  effect. \"Skip [something]\" is the same as \"Instead of doing [something], do nothing.\"…"
- **CR 500.10** (context) — "Some effects add a step after a particular phase… Any other steps that
  phase would…" (turn-structure context for step identity).

**Likely AST scope:** a static replacement whose event is a turn step (the draw step) and whose
replacement is "skip". Check GLOSSARY.md for an existing skip/step-replacement node and a turn-step
reference before inventing. **Anti-pattern:** do not model as an activated/triggered ability; it's a
continuous replacement (CR 614.10). Keep the step typed (draw step), not free text.

**Gold Input (authoritative):**
- Name: `Symbiotic Deployment` · ManaCost: `{2}{G}` · TypeLine: `Enchantment` · P/T none · Colors `[G]` · ColorIdentity `[G]`
- OracleText:
  ```
  Skip your draw step.
  {1}, Tap two untapped creatures you control: Draw a card.
  ```
