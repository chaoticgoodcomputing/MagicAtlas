## Family 1 — Equip → ActivatedAbility (delete EquipEffect)

**Failure signal:** keyword expands to `StaticAbility{Effects:[EquipEffect{Cost}]}` —
wrong ability category, cost in an Effect, attach hidden behind an opaque marker.
**Fixtures (~14):** GorgonsHead (exemplar), + 13 others matching `"EffectType":"equip"`.

**CR 702.6a (verbatim):** "Equip is an activated ability of Equipment cards. *"Equip
[cost]"* means *"[Cost]: Attach this permanent to target creature you control.
Activate only as a sorcery."*"
**CR 702.6c:** *"Equip [quality]"* restricts legal targets to a creature with the
chosen quality controlled by the activating player; the additional restriction
"don't restrict what the Equipment may be attached to."
**CR 702.6e:** "Equip planeswalker [cost]" — attach to target planeswalker you control.

**Target shape:** `ActivatedAbility{ KeywordSource:"Equip", Costs:[<equip cost>],
Effects:[AttachEffect{ Target: ObjectReference{Kind:"Target",
Filter:{CardTypes:["creature"], Controller:"You"}} }], Restrictions:["OnlyAsSorcery"],
IsManaAbility:false }`. `AttachEffect` (AST/Effects/Modification/AttachEffect.cs) is
the shared primitive — reuse it, do not synthesize a new attach node. For "Equip
[quality]" printings, narrow the filter (Subtypes/quality). Delete EquipEffect.cs.

**Anti-patterns:** keeping `Kind:"static"`; inventing the sorcery-speed restriction as
a free-text field (it's `Restrictions:["OnlyAsSorcery"]`); modeling the "while
attached" creature-status switching (engine).

## Family 2 — Cycling → ActivatedAbility (delete CyclingEffect)

**Failure signal:** `StaticAbility{Effects:[CyclingEffect{Cost}]}`.
**Fixtures (~8):** YokedPlowbeast (exemplar) + 7. Watch for **Typecycling** variants
(e.g. *…cycling*) — different effect (search, not draw).

**CR 702.29a (verbatim):** "Cycling is an activated ability that functions only while
the card with cycling is in a player's hand. *"Cycling [cost]"* means *"[Cost],
Discard this card: Draw a card."*"
**CR 702.29e (Typecycling):** *"[Type]cycling [cost]"* means *"[Cost], Discard this
card: Search your library for a [type] card, reveal it, and put it into your hand.
Then shuffle your library."*

**Target shape:** `ActivatedAbility{ KeywordSource:"Cycling", Costs:[<cost>,
DiscardCost{ self }], Effects:[DrawCardsEffect{ Count:{literal 1}, Player:{Kind:"You"}}],
IsManaAbility:false }`. DiscardCost (`CostType:"discard"`) takes Filter+Quantity —
represent "this card" as the self/this filter (check existing DiscardCost fixtures
for the self-reference idiom; if none, the smallest honest filter). The hand-only
functional restriction is engine — do NOT add a zone restriction field. Delete
CyclingEffect.cs. Typecycling: search-effect instead of DrawCardsEffect — keep
`KeywordSource:"Cycling"` (702.29f: typecycling *is* cycling).

## Family 3 — Echo → TriggeredAbility (delete EchoEffect)

**Failure signal:** `StaticAbility{Effects:[EchoEffect{Cost}]}` — Echo is *triggered*.
**Fixtures (~4):** AlbinoTroll (exemplar) + 3.

**CR 702.30a (verbatim):** "Echo is a triggered ability. *"Echo [cost]"* means *"At
the beginning of your upkeep, if this permanent came under your control since the
beginning of your last upkeep, sacrifice it unless you pay [cost]."*"

**Target shape:** `TriggeredAbility{ KeywordSource:"Echo",
Trigger:{Timing:"At", Event:"BeginningOfUpkeep", Whose:{Kind:"You"}},
InterveningIf:{Text:"this permanent came under your control since the beginning of
your last upkeep"} (CR 603.4 intervening-if → TriggeredAbility.InterveningIf),
Effects:[ PreventableEffect{ Inner: SacrificeEffect{Target:{Kind:"Self"}},
Unless:{ Player:{Kind:"Controller"}, Cost:<echo cost> } } ] }`. PreventableEffect +
UnlessClause is the existing "do X unless you pay" idiom (see corpus
`"EffectType":"preventable"`). Delete EchoEffect.cs.

**Anti-patterns:** `Kind:"static"`; echo cost in an Effect.Cost rather than the
UnlessClause; encoding the "came under control" timing as engine state instead of an
intervening-if text condition.

## Family 4 — Bestow → AlternativeCost + becomes-Aura (delete BestowEffect)

**Failure signal:** `StaticAbility{Effects:[BestowEffect{Cost}]}`.
**Fixtures (~3):** NyxbornRollicker (exemplar) + 2.

**CR 702.103a (verbatim):** "Bestow represents a static ability that functions in any
zone from which you could play the card it's on. *"Bestow [cost]"* means *"As you cast
this spell, you may choose to cast it bestowed. If you do, you pay [cost] rather than
its mana cost."* Casting a spell using its bestow ability follows the rules for paying
alternative costs."
**CR 702.103b:** cast bestowed → "becomes an Aura enchantment and gains enchant
creature."

**Target shape:** an `AlternativeCost{ Cost:<bestow cost> }` attached to the card's
cast cost (determine the host — where do spell-level alternative costs live? inspect
how the card's casting attributes carry costs; do NOT put it in Oracle.Abilities as a
pseudo-Effect), plus an honest representation of the becomes-Aura/enchant-creature
mode (an `EnchantRestrictionEffect{LegalTargets:{CardTypes:["creature"]}}` is the
existing enchant-creature primitive — see StrongBack/Gift golds). The
alternative-cast resolution, Aura-mode-toggle, and unattach rules are engine. Delete
BestowEffect.cs. **SourceSpan:** AlternativeCost carries a `required SourceSpan` that
JsonComparer does NOT ignore — set it to `{Start:0,Length:0}` in the combinator and
match in the gold (the keyword expansion has no meaningful source offset).

## Family 5 — Kicker → AdditionalCost (delete KickerEffect)

**Failure signal:** `StaticAbility{Effects:[KickerEffect{Cost}]}`.
**Fixtures (~5):** KrosanDruid (exemplar) + 4. Scope: single-cost kicker (702.33a);
Multikicker (702.33c) stays deferred.

**CR 702.33a (verbatim):** "Kicker is a static ability that functions while the spell
with kicker is on the stack. *"Kicker [cost]"* means *"You may pay an additional
[cost] as you cast this spell."* Paying a spell's kicker cost(s) follows the rules for
paying additional costs."

**Target shape:** `AdditionalCost{ Cost:<kicker cost>, IsOptional:true }` on the
card's cast cost (same host question as Bestow). The conditional resolution of kicked
effects ("if this spell was kicked, …") is a *separate linked ability* (CR 702.33e /
607) and stays as it already parses — do not couple it to the kicker cost. Delete
KickerEffect.cs. **SourceSpan:** AdditionalCost has `required SourceSpan` (NOT ignored
by JsonComparer) — set `{Start:0,Length:0}` in the combinator, match in gold.

## Family 6 — Reconfigure → two ActivatedAbilities (delete ReconfigureEffect)

**Failure signal:** `StaticAbility{Effects:[ReconfigureEffect{Cost}]}`.
**Fixtures (~2):** RabbitBattery (exemplar) + 1.

**CR 702.151a (verbatim):** "Reconfigure represents two activated abilities.
*Reconfigure [cost]* means *"[Cost]: Attach this permanent to another target creature
you control. Activate only as a sorcery"* and *"[Cost]: Unattach this permanent.
Activate only if this permanent is attached to a creature and only as a sorcery."*"
**CR 702.151b:** while attached, the Equipment stops being a creature (engine).

**Target shape:** TWO `ActivatedAbility` nodes, both `KeywordSource:"Reconfigure"`:
(1) `{Costs:[<cost>], Effects:[AttachEffect{Target: another creature you control}],
Restrictions:["OnlyAsSorcery"]}`; (2) `{Costs:[<cost>], Effects:[<unattach>],
Restrictions:["OnlyAsSorcery"]}`. AttachEffect has no detach variant yet — if an
unattach primitive is needed, add the smallest honest node OR confirm the topology
(two abilities) is the load-bearing fact and the unattach action can reuse a minimal
shape. The creature-status switching is engine. Delete ReconfigureEffect.cs.
**Note:** topology (two abilities) is itself the descriptive fact — do not collapse to one.

## Family 7 — Reference machinery (golds STAGED — match, don't edit)

Golds: **DSK/StrongBack**, **DST/LeoninShikari** (already committed at base, red).
These are the spec. Extend the parser + AST to emit exactly them.

**New AST required:**
- `CostReductionEffect.AppliesTo` — currently self-only (BasedOn/PerObject/Condition).
  StrongBack needs an ability/spell *filter* it applies to. Gold uses
  `AppliesTo:{Kind:"activatedAbility", Keyword:"Equip", Controller:"You",
  TargetsObject:{Kind:"EnchantedOrEquipped"}}` and `{Kind:"spell",
  Filter:{Subtypes:["Aura"],Controller:"You"}, TargetsObject:{...}}`.
- An **ability-reference filter** polymorphic type keyed on the `KeywordAbility` enum
  (the production/reference duality, ADR 0003: identity survives decomposition and is
  what a filter matches). Discriminators `"activatedAbility"` / `"spell"`. This same
  type is `TimingModificationEffect.AppliesTo` for LeoninShikari
  (`{Kind:"activatedAbility", Keyword:"Equip", Controller:"You"}`).
- `ObjectFilter.AttachedTo` (relational axis) — StrongBack clause 4
  `CountOf:{Subtypes:["Aura","Equipment"], AttachedTo:{Kind:"EnchantedOrEquipped"}}`.

**CR anchor (ADR 0003 follow-ups):** the keyword identity that survives decomposition
"must be the same identity a filter matches on" → typed `KeywordAbility`, not a string.
**Hot file:** touches ObjectFilter — serialized against Family 8 (do not co-merge).

## Family 8 — Exile / play-from-exile (golds STAGED — match, don't edit)

Golds: **TLA/AzulaCunningUsurper**, **TDC/TranscendentDragon**,
**ISD/CurseOfThePiercedHeart**, **TLA/TheLegendOfRoku** (committed at base, red).

**CR 406.6 (verbatim):** "An object may have one ability printed on it that causes one
or more cards to be exiled, and another ability that refers either to "the exiled
cards" or to cards "exiled with [this object]." These abilities are linked: the second
refers only to cards that have been exiled due to the first."

**ADR 0004 operational rules:** *Reference, not resolution* (encode the words "cards
exiled with Azula", do not pre-resolve the set). *Topology, not annotation* (a
persistent separate permission = a window = a separate static ability; a bundled
one-shot = nested inside the resolving ability — no flags).

**New AST required (match the staged golds exactly):**
- `MayPlayFromExile` effect (MISSING) — Azula:
  `{Cards:{Zone:"Exile", ExiledWith:{Kind:"Self"}}, Actions:["CastSpells"],
  WhoseTurn:"You", AsThoughFlash:true, ManaSpend:"anyType"}`. Static-scoped permission.
- `ObjectFilter.ExiledWith` (Azula), `.Counters` (suspend-style), `.IsToken`/
  `.ExcludedCardTypes` (Azula trigger: nontoken creature / nonland card),
  `.EntityType:"player"` (Curse enchant-player). These are reference-valued relational
  axes — same ObjectFilter as Family 7 → **serialize merges**.
- `counterSpell.ExileInsteadOfGraveyard:true` + bundled
  `OptionalEffect{Inner:castWithoutPaying{Target:{Kind:"It"}}}` (TranscendentDragon —
  the *bundled one-shot* topology, all inside one TriggeredAbility, no window).
- `ImpulseEffect.RestDestination:"RemainExiled"` + `Duration` (Roku chapter I —
  impulse with a play-window duration); `returnToBattlefield.Transformed:true` +
  `UnderControl` (Roku chapter III).
- `ObjectReference.Kind:"Choice"` + `Options[]` and `ControllerFilter`
  `"EnchantedPlayer"` (Curse damage target).
- Delete `ExileEffect.ReturnCondition` (stringly, unused; ADR 0004).

**Anti-patterns:** variable-threading from Azula's exile ability to its permission
(rejected — couples abilities whose separateness is the window); window/atomicity
flags (topology already encodes them).

## Demonstrators (golds STAGED — match, don't edit). ADR-0006 (KeywordAbilityEffect landed).

These three are red and forced by the no-ratchet land condition. Each is its own
worker (heterogeneous surfaces).

### D1 — Akroma, Angel of Wrath (C20)
Comma-separated keyword list: "Flying, first strike, vigilance, trample, haste,
protection from black and from red". Gold expects per-keyword static abilities:
`evasion` (Flying), `combatDamageTiming:{Timing:"First"}` (first strike),
`keywordAbility` (vigilance/trample/haste), and `protection{From:[{Kind:"Color",
Value:"B"},{Kind:"Color",Value:"R"}]}`. Likely gap: **protection from multiple colors**
("from black and from red") parsing into a two-element `From` array.

### D2 — Fynn, the Fangbearer (FDN)
Deathtouch keyword (parses) + triggered: "Whenever a creature you control with
deathtouch deals combat damage to a player…". Gold:
`Trigger:{Event:"DealsCombatDamageToPlayer", Filter:{CardTypes:["creature"],
Controller:"You", Characteristics:[{CharacteristicType:"keyword",
Keyword:"Deathtouch"}]}}`, `Effects:[putCounters{Target:{Kind:"ThatPlayer"},
CounterType:"poison", Count:{literal 2}}]`. Likely gaps: the combat-damage-to-player
trigger event + a **keyword characteristic in a trigger filter**.

### D3 — Gift of the Deity (EVE)
Enchant creature + two "As long as enchanted creature is [color]" conditional statics.
Gold: `StaticAbility{Condition:{Text:"enchanted creature is black"}, Effects:[modifyPT,
gainAbility(deathtouch)]}` and `{Condition:{Text:"enchanted creature is green"},
Effects:[modifyPT, allMustBlock{Target:{Kind:"EnchantedOrEquipped"}}]}`. Likely gaps:
**"as long as" conditional static** routing + `allMustBlock` effect ("all creatures
able to block it do so").
