## FAIL verdicts

### tests/magic-ast-tests/Fixtures/HandParsedCards/Vigor.json  (P14)
**Verdict:** FAIL
**Issue:** "+1/+1 counter for each 1 damage prevented this way" is modeled with `Count.DerivedFrom = "DamageDealt"`, but the damage is prevented, not dealt.
**Rule citation:** CR 615.5 (the rule the family's own parser cites)
**Rule text:** > "Some prevention effects also include an additional effect, which may refer to the amount of damage that was **prevented**."
**What the fixture says:** putCounters `"Count": { "QuantityType": "derived", "DerivedFrom": "DamageDealt" }` inside a replacement whose Replacement composite fully prevents the damage (`preventDamage All:true`).
**Why this misrepresents the rule:** The codebase's own new `DamagePrevented` DerivedKind doc (added this same batch by P15) defines `DamageDealt` as "damage that WAS dealt" and contrasts it against prevented damage ("would have been dealt but was stopped, CR 615"). Vigor deals **zero** damage to the creature — it is all prevented — so `DamageDealt` is the wrong game value. The identical oracle phrase "damage prevented this way" on Intervention Pact (P15) is correctly modeled as `DamagePrevented`; Vigor is a sibling-mislabel of the same concept within this batch.
**Suggested fix:** Change Vigor's putCounters `Count.DerivedFrom` from `"DamageDealt"` to `"DamagePrevented"` (the DerivedKind introduced by the P15 delayed-prevention-pact branch in this same batch), matching Intervention Pact's modeling of the identical phrase. Everything else on the fixture is correct.
