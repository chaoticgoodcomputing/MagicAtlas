## Process notes

Three doc-comment **citation mis-attributions** were found. All are wrong-number-for-a-
real-concept errors in explanatory parentheticals; in every case the modeling is faithful
and the load-bearing citations are correct. Per the governing threshold ("FAIL only on
absent-from-data or contradictory citation"), each cited rule is present in the data and
does **not** contradict what the node models, so none is a FAIL. They are surfaced here so
the orchestrator can correct the comments:

1. `EnchantedLandWouldBeDestroyedSacrificeGrantIndestructibleRule.cs` — attaches CR 700.4
   to "'this [object]' in an object's own text is that object", but CR 700.4 in the data
   is *"The term dies means '…put into a graveyard from the battlefield.'"* The summary
   line also cites CR 111.7 ("a token … ceases to exist") for the "this Aura" self-
   reference. The correct home for the self-reference concept is ~CR 201.4 / 109.2. The
   sacrifice-Self and indestructible modeling is nonetheless correct, and CR 614.1 /
   702.12b (the load-bearing citations) are right.

2. `EntersWithVariableCountersWhereXCountRule.cs` — cites CR 109.5 for the "other" self-
   exclusion axis (`ObjectFilter.ExcludeSelf`), but CR 109.5 is the *"you"/"your" =
   controller* rule; it says nothing about "other". CR 614.12 / 614.1c-d (load-bearing)
   are correct.

3. `ChosenTypeEntersWithCounterRule.cs` — same CR 109.5 mis-attribution for the "other"
   self-exclusion; here 109.5 is actually relevant to the card's "you control"
   (Controller=You) clause, just attached to the wrong sub-concept. CR 614.1d / 607.1
   (load-bearing) are correct.
