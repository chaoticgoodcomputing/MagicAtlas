# MAST TDD Batch 15/20 — Judge Briefing (2026-05-27)

## Family 1: (SelfConditionalCostReduction, StaticAbilityParser.Parse)

**Failure signal:** Parser bails at `StaticAbilityParser.Parse` — specifically `TryParseConditionalSpellCostReduction`, which currently only handles "during [time]" conditions. The "if you control a [Subtype]" shape falls through to the fallback.

### Cards in this family

1. **Wizard's Lightning** [DOM] — `This spell costs {2} less to cast if you control a Wizard.` (cleanliness=1)
2. **Squash** [LOR] — `This spell costs {3} less to cast if you control a Giant.` (cleanliness=1)
3. **Lookout's Dispersal** [XLN] — `This spell costs {1} less to cast if you control a Pirate.` (cleanliness=1)

### Relevant rules

- **Rule 601.2f** — Total cost to cast a spell is determined by the mana cost, plus cost increases and minus cost reductions. Cost reductions may be conditional on game state ("if you control a creature of a given type").
- **Rule 117.6 (Cost Modification)** — Cost reductions are a form of cost modification. They apply to the total cost calculation during casting. MAST models the oracle statement of the reduction and its condition; engine execution is out of scope.

### AST types in scope

- **`CostReductionEffect`** — `[OracleEffect("costReduction")]`. Fields: `Amount: Quantity`, `Condition: string?`, `PerObject: ObjectFilter?`, `BasedOn: string?`. Source: `libs/magic-ast/AST/Effects/Resource/CostReductionEffect.cs`. **Important:** `Condition` is `string?` on `CostReductionEffect`; the `StaticAbility.Condition` is `Condition?` (record with `Text`). The cost reduction sits in `Effects[0]`; the `if` condition goes on `StaticAbility.Condition`.
- **`StaticAbility`** — `[OracleAbility("static")]`. Fields: `Effects`, `Condition: Condition?`, `AffectedObjects: ObjectFilter?`. Source: `libs/magic-ast/AST/Abilities/StaticAbility.cs`.
- **`Condition`** — `{ Text: string }`. A free-text condition record. Source: `libs/magic-ast/AST/Abilities/TriggeredAbility.cs`.

### Expected generalization

One extension of `TryParseConditionalSpellCostReduction` in `StaticAbilityParser.cs` to recognise the "if you control a [SubtypeWord]" suffix, capturing the condition text and placing it on `StaticAbility.Condition`. The existing "during [time]" branch stays untouched; the new branch handles "if you control a [X]" where X is any capitalized noun phrase.

### Sibling ability notes

All three cards have a second line that is already handled by existing spell rules:
- Wizard's Lightning: `Wizard's Lightning deals 3 damage to any target.` → `SelfDealsDamageToAnyTargetRule`
- Squash: `Squash deals 6 damage to target creature or planeswalker.` → `SelfDealsDamageToTypeDisjunctionRule`
- Lookout's Dispersal: `Counter target spell unless its controller pays {4}.` → `CounterSpellRule`

### Anti-patterns

- Do **not** use `CostReductionEffect.Condition` for the "if" clause — that field is on the effect, not the ability. The `StaticAbility.Condition` is the right place per the Mental Modulation fixture.
- Do **not** try to parse the subtype into an `ObjectFilter` — the condition text is free-string at this stage; structured condition parsing is deferred per the `TODO` comment in `Condition`.
- Do **not** conflate with `TryParseTypeSpellCostReduction` ("Giant spells you cast cost {N} less") — that pattern affects OTHER spells, not THIS spell.

### Glossary gaps

None — "subtype" refers to creature subtypes (Wizard, Giant, Pirate) which are MTG domain terms covered by the broader type system.
