## FAIL verdicts

### libs/magic-ast/Parsing/Parsers/Triggered/Rules/GainLifeAndGetEnergyTriggeredRule.cs
**Verdict:** FAIL
**Issue:** Contradictory CR citation — cites "CR 701.20 (gain life)".
**Rule citation:** CR 701.20
**Rule text:** > "Reveal Example: Telepathy is an enchantment card that reads 'Your opponents play with their hands revealed.' ..." (CR 701.20 is the **Reveal** keyword action.)
**What the AST says:** doc-comment: `CR 119.3 / CR 701.20 (gain life); CR 107.14 (energy counters ...)`
**Why this misrepresents the rule:** CR 701.20 is the Reveal keyword action, unrelated to life gain. Life gain is CR 119.3 (correctly co-cited); there is no "gain life" keyword action in section 701. The 701.20 citation contradicts the modeled effect (GainLifeEffect + GainEnergyEffect).
**Suggested fix:** Drop `CR 701.20` from the gain-life citation; CR 119.3 alone is correct (optionally add CR 118 for life as a resource). The node's modeling and its 107.14/122.1 energy citations are fine.
