- `tests/magic-ast-tests/Fixtures/HandParsedCards/VexingSphinx.json` (M08) — PASS.
  Flying (evasion) + cumulative-upkeep discard-cost + dies payoff. The cumulative-upkeep triggered
  ability is fully decomposed per CR 702.24a: `putCounters age +1 on Self`, then
  `PreventableEffect{ Inner: sacrifice Self, Unless: pay ScaledCost(PerUnit=discard 1 card,
  Count=CounterCountQuantity age On:Self) }` — the "for each age counter" scaling and "sacrifice
  unless you pay" are structural, not free text. The whitelisted `OtherCondition` intervening-if
  ("this permanent is on the battlefield") is GENUINE: it is literally the intervening-if clause in
  CR 702.24a ("At the beginning of your upkeep, if this permanent is on the battlefield, ...") and
  matches the established InnerSanctum (draw-cost) / PsychicVortex (life-cost) precedent — both use
  the identical `OtherCondition` shape and the same PB-7 structured-condition whitelist rationale at
  base. Accounted debt, not a fidelity FAIL. Dies trigger → `drawCards` with
  `CounterCountQuantity age On:Self` (reference-not-resolution). The new own-file
  `CumulativeUpkeepDiscardCardStaticRule` is anchored on the em-dash "Discard" template and does not
  touch `CumulativeUpkeepPayLifeStaticRule` (the rule reused elsewhere in the batch) — no collision.

- `tests/magic-ast-tests/Fixtures/HandParsedCards/Psychatog.json` (M10) — PASS.
  Both activated abilities structured. Pump 1: `DiscardCost{card, 1}` → `modifyPT Self +1/+1 until
  end of turn`. Pump 2: untyped `ExileCost{CardTypes:["card"], Controller:You, FromZone:Graveyard,
  Quantity:2}` → same `modifyPT`. Exile-as-cost sits in `Costs[]` of an activated ability — CR
  118.8a (costs) / CR 602.1a (the activation cost is everything before the colon). The new
  `ExileCardsFromGraveyardUntypedCostRule` is anchored `^Exile N cards from your graveyard$` with no
  type-word capture; verified disjoint from the typed sibling `ExileCardsFromGraveyardCostRule`
  (whose regex requires a type word before "cards", so it cannot match "two cards", and the untyped
  regex cannot match "three creature cards"), and it cannot steal "exile this/that card" (not in the
  count alternation, no "from your graveyard" tail). No new discriminator (reuses `ExileCost` /
  `ModifyPTEffect` / `DiscardCost`).
  Note: the doc-comment cites CR 118.8a (additional costs); the precise "cost before the colon" rule
  is CR 602.1a. The citation is imprecise but does not contradict the modeling (exile is a valid
  cost) and reuses the identical citation already accepted on the sibling rule at base — not a FAIL.
