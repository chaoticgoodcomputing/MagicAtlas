## Per-family verdicts

### P03 enchanted-loses-abilities-dont-untap (Stop Cold) — PASS
Card fully structured: Flash (`timingModification` Grant Instant), Enchant artifact-or-creature
(`enchantRestriction` CardTypes disjunction), ETB `tap` EnchantedOrEquipped, and a single static
ability carrying the composite `loseAbility` + `doesntUntap` on the same subject. `AbilityText:
"all abilities"` is the intended use of the reused `LoseAbilityEffect.AbilityText` `[FreeTextField]`,
whose contract explicitly reserves it for ability SCOPES ("all abilities"/"all other abilities") vs
bare keywords (`Keyword`). Whitelist entry is precedent-consistent with `VraskaBetrayalsSting` ("all
other abilities") and `AnimateDead` (PB-9 LoseAbilityEffect residual, burndown-tagged debt). Cited
CR 502.3 (untap step — effects can keep permanents from untapping) and CR 611.3 (static ability
generates a continuous effect) are verbatim-exact; CR 613 (interaction of continuous effects, layer 6
ability removal) is a valid section-level cite, not contradictory. `WhoseUntapStep:"its controller's"`
is a pre-existing reused-node string (correctly distinguishing controller-of-enchanted vs Aura
controller) and out of this branch's scope.

### P09 becomes-tapped-proliferate (Kilo, Apogee Mind) — PASS
Haste `keywordAbility` + triggered `BecomesTapped` with `IsSelf:true` and reused `proliferate` effect
(verbatim reminder text, exempt). Self-by-name matcher `SelfByNameBecomesTappedConditionRule`:
anchored `^[A-Z]…` (only capitalized name-subjects), priority 983 below generic (985) and subtype
(984), gate on "becomes tapped" + leading-timing strip. Sibling sweep: lowercase openers ("a"/"an"/
"this creature") fail the `^[A-Z]` anchor, so generic/subtype shapes are never poached; the shared
`TriggeredRuleHelpers` was left untouched. `CardTypes:["creature"]` default is correct (Kilo is a
creature; `SelfReferenceTypeCorrector` retypes otherwise). CR 603.2 (becomes-tapped trigger, verbatim
example present) + CR 201.5 (card naming itself) + CR 701.34 (Proliferate) all present.

### P11 creature-to-graveyard-trigger (Mortuary) — PASS
"a creature is put into your graveyard from the battlefield" -> `Dies` + `Controller:You` is faithful
(CR 700.4 verbatim: dies = "is put into a graveyard from the battlefield"; naming your graveyard
restricts to creatures you own). `putOnTopOfLibrary` Target `It` correctly back-references "that card".
Sibling sweep: the condition regex requires literal `a\s+creature…$`, so the nontoken sibling surface
("a nontoken creature is put into your graveyard…") never matches; anchored `$` blocks compound-clause
poaching. CR 111.1 (tokens) cited for the sibling distinction, present.

### P12 opponent-draws-second-trigger (Faerie Mastermind) — PASS
Flash + Flying reused; triggered `DrawsCard` / `Controller:Opponent` / `Ordinal:2` / `PerTurn:true`
("an opponent draws their second card each turn -> you draw a card"), and the {3}{U} activated
`drawCards` `EachPlayer`. Worker cited CR 121.1 ("A player draws a card by putting the top card of
their library into their hand") — section 121 is titled "Drawing a Card", matching the in-repo
`OpponentDrawsCardConditionRule` precedent; the brief's tentative CR 120.x is superseded. CR 603.2
present. `EachPlayerDrawsCardEffectRule` anchored, priority 999 above the generic "you" fallback.

### P19 aggregate-cards-to-graveyard-trigger (Thran Vigil) — PASS
"one or more artifact and/or creature cards leave your graveyard during your turn" -> `LeavesGraveyard`
+ `CardTypes:["artifact","creature"]` (structured list-as-disjunction, not free text) + `Controller:You`

decision is required. Anchored regex prevents collision with the untyped Quintorius sibling. CR 603.2
verbatim-exact.
