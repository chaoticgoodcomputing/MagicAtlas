## FAIL verdicts

### libs/magic-ast/Parsing/Parsers/Static/Rules/CantBlockRule.cs  (self-cant-block, F11)
**Verdict:** FAIL
**Issue:** SIBLING-MISLABEL OVERFIT — the widened self-by-name regex mislabels board-wide/typed blocking restrictions as a self-restriction.
**Rule citation:** CR 509.1b (blocking restrictions), CR 201.5 (name in own text = that object)
**Rule text:** > "The defending player checks each creature they control to see whether it's affected by any restrictions (effects that say a creature can't block…)."
**What the AST says:** the new arm
`^\s*(?!This|Enchanted|Equipped|Target|Each|All|Other|Any|That)[A-Z][^,\n]+?\s+can'?t\s+block\.?\s*$`
emits a bare `CantBlockEffect()` (Target=null ⇒ Self) for any capitalized, comma-free subject.
**Why this misrepresents the rule:** corpus grep of `oracle-cards.json` shows the regex matches 8 real cards whose subject is NOT the card's own name — a mass or typed restriction — each collapsed to "this card can't block":
- Bedlam (Enchantment) — "Creatures can't block."
- Tazeem (Plane) — "Creatures can't block."
- Razorjaw Oni — "Black creatures can't block."
- Frenetic Raptor — "Beasts can't block."
- Siege Elemental — "Untapped creatures can't block."
- Magistrate's Veto (Enchantment) — "White creatures and blue creatures can't block."
- Bothersome Quasit — "Goaded creatures your opponents control can't block."
- Spitting Dilophosaurus — "Creatures your opponents control with -1/-1 counters on them can't block."
`AbilityClassifier` defaults these restriction lines to Static (classifier comment: "Without these rules the classifier defaults to Static"), no higher-priority static rule and no filtered static emitter handles them, so CantBlockRule (priority 959) steals them. For the two non-creature enchantments (Bedlam, Magistrate's Veto) "this card can't block" is nonsensical; for the typed cards it silently drops the whole subject filter (all black creatures / all Beasts / opponents' goaded creatures) and re-scopes the restriction to the source object.
**Suggested fix:** narrow the self-by-name arm so it only fires on a genuine proper-noun self-reference — e.g. require the subject to equal the card's own (short) name, or restrict to a single leading Capitalized token that is not a creature type / color / state word, and exclude any subject containing "creature(s)". The exclusion list (This/Enchanted/Equipped/Target/Each/All/Other/Any/That) does not cover Creatures/Black/White/Beasts/Untapped/Goaded and every other typed subject. The Norin fixture itself is correct and would still parse.
