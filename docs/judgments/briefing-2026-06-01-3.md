## F01 — "All creatures have [keyword]" static team grant (Mass Hysteria)
- **Signal:** `StaticAbilityParser.Parse`, DY 5. Line "All creatures have haste." unparsed.
- **CR 611.3** (verbatim): "A continuous effect may be generated by the static ability of an object. Example: A permanent with the static ability 'All white creatures get +1/+1' generates an effect that continuously gives +1/+1 to each white creature on the battlefield…"
- **CR 702.10a** (verbatim): "Haste is a static ability."
- **AST:** reuse `GainAbilityEffect` (no Duration — permanent static). Subject is an `ObjectFilter` over **all creatures** (no controller restriction). New static rule. Mirror the existing self/target keyword-grant rules; only the subject (all creatures, unrestricted) differs.
- **Anti-patterns:** don't add a node; don't restrict the subject to "you control"; no Duration.

## F02 — "Equipped creature gets +N/+N and can't block" static (Copper Carapace)
- **Signal:** DY 6. Line "Equipped creature gets +2/+2 and can't block." unparsed (Equip line already parses).
- **CR 613.4c** (verbatim): "Layer 7c: Effects and counters that modify power and/or toughness (but don't set power and/or toughness to a specific number or value) are applied."
- **CR 509.1a** (verbatim, excerpt): "The defending player chooses which creatures they control, if any, will block…" — "can't block" removes the equipped creature from this choice.
- **AST:** a **static** ability (no Duration) carrying TWO effects (multi-effect clause): `ModifyPTEffect` (subject `EnchantedOrEquipped`, +2/+2) and `CantBlockEffect` (same subject). Both nodes exist. New static rule.
- **Anti-patterns:** no Duration (this is a static grant, not "until end of turn"); don't free-text "can't block"; don't add a node.

## F03 — Rally/Ally team ETB-grant trigger (Chasm Guide, Ondu Champion)
- **Signal:** `StaticAbilityParser.Parse`, DY 7. "Rally — Whenever this creature or another Ally you control enters, creatures you control gain [kw] until end of turn."
- **CR 207.2c** (verbatim, excerpt): "An ability word appears in italics at the beginning of some abilities… they have no special rules meaning… The ability words are adamant, addendum, alliance, battalion, bloodrush, celebration…" (Rally is in this class — peeled into `AbilityWord`, no rules meaning.)
- **CR 603.2** (verbatim, excerpt): "Whenever a game event or game state matches a triggered ability's trigger event, that ability automatically triggers…"
- **CR 611.1** (verbatim): "A continuous effect modifies characteristics of objects, modifies control of objects, or affects players or the rules of the game, for a fixed or indefinite period."
- **AST:** a triggered ability; the "Rally —" prefix is captured as `AbilityWord` (ability-word handling already exists — see GLOSSARY). Trigger event: a creature entering, filtered to **this creature OR another Ally you control**. Effect: `GainAbilityEffect` (the keyword) on subject "creatures you control", `Duration` until end of turn. New triggered rule. Verify the "this or another [subtype] you control" enter-filter shape against existing ETB-trigger rules.
- **Anti-patterns:** don't treat "Rally" as a keyword with rules meaning; don't drop the duration; the granted keyword is structured, not free text.

## F05 — Count-based self-pump "+N/+0 for each other X you control" (Nim Lasher, Benalish Honor Guard)
