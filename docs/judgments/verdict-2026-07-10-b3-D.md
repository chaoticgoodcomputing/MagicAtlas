## FAIL verdicts

### tests/magic-ast-tests/Fixtures/HandParsedCards/MagebaneArmor.json (H19 — equipped-pt-loses-keyword)
**Verdict:** FAIL
**Issue:** `LoseAbilityEffect.AbilityText: "flying"` inlines a single evergreen keyword as free text.
**Rule citation:** CR 611.2 (continuous effect removing an ability); modeling context CR 702.6 (Equip)
**Rule text:** > "A continuous effect may be generated by the resolution of a spell or ability." (611.2); Equip attaches to a creature (702.6).
**What the fixture says:** `{"EffectType":"loseAbility","Target":{"Kind":"EnchantedOrEquipped"},"AbilityText":"flying"}`
**Why this misrepresents the rule:** Per the SKILL, a `*Text` field holding a value a typed AST node can capture is an escape-hatch FAIL, and "the bar is *could* a structured node express this, NOT does one already exist." "flying" is a single evergreen keyword: `KeywordAbility.Flying` is in the enum, and the corpus already models keywords structurally as `Characteristic{CharacteristicType:"keyword", Keyword:"Flying"}` (used pervasively in the very Flying evasion filters elsewhere in this batch). So a structured node demonstrably expresses it — the free-text string is a shortcut, invisible to downstream consumers (can't be queried as "removes keyword X"), and prone to casing/spelling drift. The two cited precedents are off-point: AnimateDead's `"enchant creature card in a graveyard"` and VraskaBetrayalsSting's `"all other abilities"` are genuinely-unstructurable ability-*scopes*, not single keywords. Across the entire base corpus, `loseAbility` appears only twice — both ability-scope references, never a bare structurable keyword. This is the first time a structurable keyword is laundered through the free-text whitelist, so the "designed field / precedent" justification does not hold for this value.
**Suggested fix:** Give `LoseAbilityEffect` a structured keyword slot and model "loses flying" as a keyword reference — e.g. reuse the existing keyword `Characteristic` (`{CharacteristicType:"keyword", Keyword:"Flying"}`) or a `KeywordAbility` field — rather than the free-text `AbilityText`. Surface the "loses [keyword]" shape to the de-string / PB-9 design initiative. (The rest of the branch — ModifyPT +2/+4 on EnchantedOrEquipped, the single-static-ability-two-effects encoding, `PreventDamageEffect{All, NoncombatOnly}` mirroring `CombatOnly`, and the anchored classifier intercept — is correct and would PASS on its own.)
