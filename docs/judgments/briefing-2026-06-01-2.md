## F1 — Activated pump of the enchanted/equipped creature (cluster 4)

- **Failure signal:** `DominantLastAttemptedRule: null`, `FractionalYield 10.33`, `DirectYield 9`. The activated-ability wrapper parses; the effect `Enchanted/Equipped creature gets +N/+M until end of turn` is the unparsed line.
- **Cards:** Blessing (`{W}: Enchanted creature gets +1/+1 until end of turn`), Demonspine Whip (`{X}: Equipped creature gets +X/+0 until end of turn`).
- **Relevant rules:**
  - **CR 611.1** (verbatim): "A continuous effect modifies characteristics of objects, modifies control of objects, or affects players or the rules of the game, for a fixed or indefinite period."
  - **CR 613.4c** (verbatim): "Layer 7c: Effects and counters that modify power and/or toughness (but don't set power and/or toughness to a specific number or value) are applied."
  - **CR 208.1** (verbatim): "…Power and toughness can be modified or set to particular values by effects."
- **AST in scope (pointer, not whitelist):** `ModifyPTEffect` (existing), subject `ObjectReference{Kind: EnchantedOrEquipped}` (existing), `Duration` → until-end-of-turn (existing). The boost amounts are `PowerToughnessValue` — fixed (`+1/+1`) and **variable** (`+X/+0`, where the `{X}` cost binds X). Use the existing variable-PT representation; do not invent a new one for X.
- **Generalization:** an activated ability on an Aura/Equipment whose effect is a temporary P/T modification of the attached creature. Same shape as existing target/self pump rules, only the subject differs (`EnchantedOrEquipped`).
- **Anti-patterns:** don't add a new node — `ModifyPTEffect` already models this. Don't free-text the subject. Don't model the `{X}` as a literal number.

## F2 — Static keyword grant to the enchanted/equipped creature (cluster 15)

- **Failure signal:** `DominantLastAttemptedRule: StaticAbilityParser.Parse`, `FractionalYield 7.67`, `DirectYield 5`. The static line `Enchanted/Equipped creature has [kw] and [kw]` is unparsed.
- **Cards:** Messenger's Speed (`Enchanted creature has trample and haste`), Fleetfeather Sandals (`Equipped creature has flying and haste`).
- **Relevant rules:**
  - **CR 611.1** (verbatim): see F1.
  - **CR 611.3** (verbatim, excerpt): "A continuous effect may be generated by the static ability of an object. Example: A permanent with the static ability 'All white creatures get +1/+1' generates an effect that continuously gives +1/+1 to each white creature on the battlefield…"
  - **CR 702.5a** (verbatim): "Enchant is a static ability, written 'Enchant [object or player].' The enchant ability restricts what an Aura spell can target and what an Aura can enchant."
- **AST in scope:** `GainAbilityEffect` (existing; `GainedAbility` is singular). Model `has trample and haste` as a static ability with **two** `GainAbilityEffect` children (multi-effect per clause), subject `EnchantedOrEquipped`. This is a *static* grant — **no Duration** (persists while attached), distinct from the existing activated `GainAbilityEffectRule` ("gains [kw] until end of turn"). The granted keyword uses the shared keyword enum (the same `KeywordAbility` identity referenced by `GainAbilityEffect.GainedAbility`).
- **Generalization:** static "[attached subject] has [kw](s)" → one `GainAbilityEffect` per keyword.
- **Anti-patterns:** don't reuse the activated rule (wrong timing/duration semantics). Don't fold two keywords into a free-text string. Don't add a Duration.

## F3 — "Becomes the creature type of your choice until end of turn" (cluster 16) — JUDGE

- **Failure signal:** `DominantLastAttemptedRule: null`, `FractionalYield 7.5`, `DirectYield 7`. Activated effect `This creature becomes the creature type of your choice until end of turn` is unparsed.
- **Cards:** Mistform Stalker (`{1}: This creature becomes the creature type of your choice until end of turn`; its 2nd ability already parses), Mistform Dreamer (same line; `Flying` parses).
- **Relevant rules:**
  - **CR 613.1d** (verbatim): "Layer 4: Type-changing effects are applied. These include effects that change an object's card type, subtype, and/or supertype."
  - **CR 205.3** (verbatim, excerpt): "…When choosing a creature type, 'Merfolk' or 'Wizard' is acceptable, but 'Merfolk Wizard' is not. Words like 'artifact,' 'opponent,' 'Swamp,' or 'truck' can't be chosen because they aren't creature types."
  - **CR 205.1a** (verbatim, excerpt): "…when an effect sets one or more of an object's subtypes, the new subtype(s) replaces any existing subtypes from the appropriate set (creature types, land types, …)."
- **AST in scope:** `ChangeSubtypeEffect` (existing) currently has `Target` + literal `Subtypes` (list of strings) and **no Duration**. This card needs (a) a **chosen** creature type rather than a literal — there is precedent vocabulary (`ChosenCharacteristicKind.CreatureType`, `ChooseCreatureTypeEffect`, `ObjectFilter.ChosenCharacteristic`); the "of your choice" here is a fresh on-resolution choice, not a reference to a prior as-enters choice, so weigh whether it's a chosen-type flag on `ChangeSubtypeEffect` vs. a small composite — and (b) a `Duration` (until end of turn). `ChangeSubtypeEffect : ContinuousEffect`, so Duration may already be available on the base — confirm before adding a field.
- **Generalization:** "[subject] becomes the creature type of your choice [until end of turn]" — one continuous type-changing effect with a player-chosen subtype and an optional duration. Decide the shape (you own it); keep it one faithful node for one grammatical clause, per the `BecomesCreatureEffect` rationale.
- **Anti-patterns:** don't encode "of your choice" as a free-text subtype string (`"your choice"` is not a creature type). Don't reach for `BecomesCreatureEffect` (that's *becomes a creature*; this stays a creature and only sets its creature type). Don't drop the duration.

## F4 — Manland animate: leading-duration + "still a land" (cluster 13) — JUDGE

- **Failure signal:** `DominantLastAttemptedRule: null`, `FractionalYield 8`, `DirectYield 7`. Line: `Until end of turn, this land becomes a N/M [colors] [type] creature with [kw]. It's still a land.`
- **Cards:** Stirring Wildwood (`…becomes a 3/4 green and white Elemental creature with reach. It's still a land.`; clean), Restless Vents (same animate shape + an attack trigger that already parses + menace).
- **Relevant rules:**
  - **CR 611.1** (verbatim): see F1.
  - **CR 205.1b** (verbatim, excerpt): "Some effects change an object's card type, supertype, or subtype but specify that the object retains a prior card type, supertype, or subtype. In such cases, all the object's prior card types, supertypes, and subtypes are retained. This rule applies to effects that … state that something is 'still a [type…]'…"
  - **CR 305.7** (verbatim, excerpt): "…Setting a land's subtype doesn't add or remove any card types (such as creature) or supertypes… If a land gains one or more land types in addition to its own, it keeps its land types and rules text…"
- **AST in scope:** `BecomesCreatureEffect` (existing — the Keyrune animate template, fields: P/T, Colors, AddedSubtypes, CardTypes, GainedAbilities, Duration). The Keyrune form puts duration **last** ("…with flying until end of turn"); the manland puts it **first** ("Until end of turn, this land becomes…"). The gap is the **rule's word-order handling**, plus consuming the trailing **"It's still a land"** reminder. Per CR 205.1b the animate is additive — CardTypes should include `creature` added to land; "still a land" is the reminder confirming the land type is retained (model it via additive CardTypes, not a new field; do not emit an unparsed node for that sentence).
- **Generalization:** accept leading-duration on the becomes-creature line and treat "It's still a land" as the additive-retention reminder it is. The activated wrapper `{cost}: …` already parses.
- **Anti-patterns:** don't create a second animate node — extend the existing rule. Don't leave "It's still a land" as `UnparsedEffect`. Don't drop the menace/reach keyword into free text.
