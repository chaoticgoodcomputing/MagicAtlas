**Usage-driven, not triage-yield-driven.** Targets cards in the **top ~250 EDH combos** (Commander Spellbook, popularity-sorted) that MAST does **not** yet fully parse (`ParsedAbilities < TotalAbilities`). 206 of 280 top-combo cards are incomplete; these 8 families cover the highest-combo-frequency ones. ## F1 — "Whenever an opponent loses life, you gain that much life" (Exquisite Blood [6 combos], Bloodthirsty Conqueror) — JUDGE
- **CR 603.2** (triggered) + **CR 119.3:** "If an effect causes a player to gain life or lose life, that player's life total is adjusted accordingly."
- **AST:** a `TriggeredAbility`, trigger `TriggerCondition{ Event: LosesLife, <an opponent> }` (the `LosesLife` event exists), effect `GainLifeEffect` whose amount is "that much" = the life just lost — a derived quantity (`DerivedQuantity{ DerivedFrom: LifeLost }`). Subject "you". Strip the reminder. Read GLOSSARY for `LosesLife` trigger + `DerivedQuantity.LifeLost`.
- **Anti-patterns:** "that much" is the derived life-lost quantity, not a literal/free-text; the trigger filters to *an opponent*; no new node.

## F2 — "You may play lands from your graveyard" static (Crucible of Worlds [4 combos], Ramunap Excavator) — sonnet
- **CR 305.1** (verbatim, excerpt): "A player who has priority may play a land card from their hand during a main phase of their turn when the stack is empty. Playing a land is a special action…"
- **AST:** a static permission. `MayPlayFromExileEffect` exists for the exile-zone analogue (read it — it has a "what it permits" + presumably a zone/filter). Either extend it to a `Graveyard` source with a land filter, or a sibling play-permission. Subject = land cards in your graveyard. No execution semantics (the actual play is engine territory).
- **Anti-patterns:** don't free-text; reuse the play-permission node/pattern; the zone is graveyard, the filter is lands.

## F3 — "You may cast this card from your graveyard as long as [condition]" (Gravecrawler [13 combos — top target]) — JUDGE
- **CR 601.3e** (verbatim, excerpt): "Some rules and effects state that an alternative set of characteristics or a subset of characteristics are considered to determine if a card or copy of a card is legal to cast…"
- **AST:** a cast-permission-from-graveyard with a condition. `AlternativeCastEffect` carries a `Zone` (Graveyard — see its doc: Flashback/Escape/Aftermath cast from graveyard) — model "cast this from your graveyard" with that, plus the gating condition "as long as you control a Zombie" (a `Condition`/`CountCondition` over Zombies you control). ("This creature can't block" already parses.) Read `AlternativeCastEffect.cs` + the `Condition` union.
- **Anti-patterns:** the condition "you control a Zombie" is a structured `Condition`, not free text; subject is this card (Self) from Graveyard; don't drop the condition.

## F4 — "{T}: Draw a card, then put this artifact on top of its owner's library" (Sensei's Divining Top [10 combos]) — JUDGE
- **CR 121.1** (verbatim, excerpt): "A player draws a card by putting the top card of their library into their hand…"
- **AST:** an activated ability `{T}:` with two effects (multi-effect): `DrawCardsEffect` (1) then `PutOnTopOfLibraryEffect` (exists — read it) with subject Self (this artifact → top of its owner's library). ("{1}: Look at top three…" already parses.) Read `PutOnTopOfLibraryEffect` + `DrawCardsEffect`.
- **Anti-patterns:** two structured effects in order; "this artifact" → Self; "its owner's library" top is the destination, not free text.

## F5 — "Whenever this creature or another creature dies, target player loses N and you gain N" (Blood Artist [4 combos]) — sonnet
- **CR 700.4:** "The term dies means 'is put into a graveyard from the battlefield.'" + **CR 603.2** (triggered).
- **AST:** a `TriggeredAbility`, trigger = a creature **dying** filtered to "this creature or another creature" (the self-inclusive creature filter — mirror the existing "this or another [type]" enter-filter, but for the Dies event), effects = `LoseLifeEffect`(target player, N) + `GainLifeEffect`(you, N) (multi-effect, the drain shape from batch-5 F11). Read GLOSSARY for the Dies trigger event + the self-inclusive filter.
- **Anti-patterns:** two structured drain effects; the trigger filter is "this or another creature" (self-inclusive), not just "another"; "target player" is targeted.

## F6 — "When this creature enters, copy target instant or sorcery spell. You may choose new targets for the copy." (Dualcaster Mage [6 combos]) — JUDGE
- **CR 707.10** (verbatim, excerpt): "To copy a spell, activated ability, or triggered ability means to put a copy of it onto the stack; a copy of a spell isn't cast…"
- **AST:** an ETB `TriggeredAbility` whose effect is `CopyEffect` (exists — read it) targeting an instant-or-sorcery spell on the stack, with the "may choose new targets" option captured. (Flash already parses.) Read `CopyEffect.cs` for its target/new-targets fields.
- **Anti-patterns:** reuse `CopyEffect`; the target is "target instant or sorcery spell" (a spell filter), structured; the "may choose new targets" is a structured flag, not free text.

## F7 — "Landfall — Whenever a land you control enters, create a Food token or a Treasure token" (Tireless Provisioner [5 combos]) — JUDGE
- **CR 603.2** (triggered) + **CR 111.10a/b:** "A Treasure token is a colorless Treasure artifact token with '{T}, Sacrifice this token: Add one mana of any color.'" / "A Food token is a colorless Food artifact token with '{2}, {T}, Sacrifice this token: You gain 3 life.'"
- **AST:** a landfall `TriggeredAbility` (Landfall ability-word peeled; trigger = "a land you control enters") whose effect is a **modal choice** between two `CreateTokenEffect`s — `TokenDefinition.Food()` or `TokenDefinition.Treasure()` (both EXIST). Model the "create X or Y" as a `ModalEffect` (choose one) of two create-token effects, OR the cleanest faithful "create a [Food|Treasure]" choice shape — you decide. Strip the parenthetical token reminders. Read `TokenDefinition` (Food/Treasure) + `CreateTokenEffect` + `ModalEffect`.
- **Anti-patterns:** "Food or Treasure" is a CHOICE (modal), not two creates; reuse the predefined `TokenDefinition.Food()/.Treasure()`; don't free-text the token text (it's reminder).

## F8 — "If an opponent would mill one or more cards, they mill twice that many cards instead" (Bruvac the Grandiloquent [6 combos]) — JUDGE
- **CR 614.1** (verbatim, excerpt): "Some continuous effects are replacement effects… replacement effects apply continuously as events happen… Such effects watch for a particular event…" + **CR 701.17a:** "For a player to mill a number of cards, that player puts that many cards from the top of their library into their graveyard."
- **AST:** a `ReplacementEffect` (exists — read it + `DrawReplacementRule` for the precedent shape): the replaced event is "an opponent would mill ≥1 cards"; the replacement is "mill twice that many instead". Model the event (a mill of N by an opponent) and the modifier (double the amount). Look at `DrawReplacementRule`/`ReplacementEffect`/`ReplacementModifier` for how a "would do X, do Y instead" doubling is structured.
- **Anti-patterns:** this is a `ReplacementEffect`, not a triggered ability ("would … instead" = replacement, CR 614); "twice that many" is a structured doubling modifier, not free text; the affected player is an opponent.
