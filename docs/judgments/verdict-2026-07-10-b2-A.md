## Process notes

1. **Sibling generalization is clean, not overfit (G04).** Grepping `oracle-cards.json`:
   - "Spells you cast from anywhere other than your hand cost {N} less to cast" is shared by Advanced
     Reconstruction ({2}), Bilbo ({1}), Fortune Teller's Talent ({2}), Sage of the Beyond ({2}) — all
     the exact template; the rule captures `{N}` via `(?<amount>\d+)` and generalizes correctly.
   - "exile target permanent card from your graveyard" is unique to Savvy Trader.
   - "you may play that card for as long as it remains exiled" is shared widely; the two clean siblings
     (Praetor's Grasp, Valakut Exploration) would parse correctly, and the mana-rider siblings
     (Court of Locthwain, Gonti, Cunning Rhetoric) are protected from mislabel by the `^…$` anchor +
     the dispatcher's period-delimited sentence splitting — their rider is comma-joined in the same
     sentence, so the anchor never matches. No mislabel risk.

2. **Two inaccurate "verbatim" doc quotes in G04 (not FAILs, but worth fixing).** Both cite an
   existing, non-contradictory rule *number*, so under the judge doctrine (FAIL only on
   absent-from-data or contradiction) they PASS — but the quoted prose is wrong:
   - `ObjectInZoneCondition.cs` cites CR 611.2c "verbatim, excerpt" with text ("If the effect is
     conditional … the effect stops applying …") that appears nowhere in the rules data. The on-point
     subrule for a "for as long as" duration is **611.2b** ("Some continuous effects … have durations
     worded 'for as long as …'"). 611.2c is actually about the affected-object set being fixed — off
     topic but not contradictory. Recommend re-citing 611.2b and correcting the quote.
   - `SpellsCastFromNonHandZoneCostReductionRule.cs` cites CR 601.2f "verbatim, excerpt" with text that
     does not match 601.2f. The *number* is correct (601.2f genuinely covers cost reduction: "…minus
     all cost reductions"), only the quoted excerpt is wrong. Recommend swapping in the real 601.2f text.

3. **Pre-existing convention, out of scope, flagged for the engine-lens audit (G14).**
   "create a token that's a copy of that card and that's tapped and attacking" is modelled as a bare
   `CopyEffect{Target:It}`; the "tapped and attacking" status is dropped. This follows the established,
   documented convention (`CreateTappedAttackingCopyOfNontokenCreatureRule` / Satya + Kari Zev gold
   fixtures) — `CopyEffect` carries no tapped/attacking field. Since the branch faithfully reuses the
   existing convention the dispatch told it to reuse, this is not a per-item FAIL; it is an AST-family
   gap for `docs/ast-engine-lens-audit.md` if the tapped/attacking status ever matters to a flow rule.
   Likewise the `It` anaphora resolves to two different antecedents (the exiled card for `copy`, the
   created token for the delayed `exile`) — an established reference convention; resolution is engine
   territory, out of judge scope.
